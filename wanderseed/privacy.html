Wanderseed privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version. Wanderseed has no accounts and asks for no personal details. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows optional adverts through Google AdMob, set up for children: no personalised ads and no advertising ID.

Who we are

Wanderseed is a mobile game made by Werner Hübsch, an independent developer. Questions about this policy can be sent to wanderseed.game@gmail.com.

Who the game is for

Wanderseed is made for everyone, including children under 13. Because children may play, the game treats every player as a child. It does not ask for anyone's age.

What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves the following on your device only. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Your progress: best score and distance, drops, missions, garden plants and unlocked seed colours.
  • The player name and character you pick. The name is only shown on your own screen.
  • Your settings, such as whether sound is on.
  • A record of your most recent runs: when each was played, how far it went and how it ended.

You can erase all of it by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the game.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no sign-in, no online leaderboards, no chat, no purchases and no analytics of our own. We do not receive any information about you or your device.

Adverts

The game offers optional video adverts in exchange for a reward, such as carrying on after a bump. An advert plays only when you tap its button. The adverts come from Google AdMob.

Every advert request is marked as directed at children and at users under the age of consent. This means:

  • Adverts are not personalised. They are not based on your interests or on what you do in other apps.
  • The game does not ask for your device's advertising ID.
  • Adverts are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

To deliver an advert and to prevent fraud, Google receives some technical information straight from your device, such as its IP address (which shows an approximate location), the device model, operating system version and language, and whether an advert was shown or tapped. Google handles this under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More on how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Adverts may show other apps or products. Tapping an advert opens it outside the game, where this policy no longer applies.

Permissions

The game uses internet access and network state so adverts can load. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files. The game can be played without a connection; adverts are simply not offered.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not hold any. The only other party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Parents and guardians

If you have a question about your child's use of the game, or want help erasing the game's data from a device, write to wanderseed.game@gmail.com. Since we hold no data about players, there is nothing on our side to look up or delete; requests about data Google holds go to Google.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change before the new version of the game is released.
